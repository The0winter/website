# Ranking font

The bundled WOFF2 contains the seven Chinese characters used by the ranking navigation.
It is a subset of Adobe Source Han Sans SC Medium (weight 500), with unchanged glyph outlines.
The internal family and PostScript names were changed to Jiutian Ranking Sans to respect the reserved font name.
